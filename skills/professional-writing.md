# Professional Writing

Skill for polished workplace, business, formal, and client-facing communication.

## Use for
- emails and formal messages
- reports, proposals, briefs, documentation
- professional profiles and announcements
- customer/client communication

## Rules
- Lead with the purpose and requested action.
- Match formality to audience and relationship.
- Be precise, respectful, and concise without sounding robotic.
- State dates, owners, decisions, and next steps clearly when relevant.
- Avoid buzzword-heavy corporate language.
- Preserve confidentiality and do not invent business facts.

## Output checks
Confirm the draft has a clear purpose, logical structure, appropriate tone, concrete details, and a clean close.
