# Academic Writing

Skill for school and academic work where accuracy, structure, evidence discipline, and rubric alignment matter.

## Use for
- essays and long answers
- reports and research write-ups
- analytical responses
- summaries and explanations
- source-based assignments

## Rules
- Follow the requested academic level and subject vocabulary.
- Separate facts, interpretation, and opinion.
- Never fabricate citations, quotations, experiments, or evidence.
- Use clear thesis → evidence/explanation → conclusion structure when appropriate.
- Preserve the student's intended argument while improving organization and language.
- Respect word limits, formatting requirements, and citation style when supplied.

## Final check
Check factual support, logical progression, terminology, grammar, citation integrity, and whether every part of the prompt is answered.
