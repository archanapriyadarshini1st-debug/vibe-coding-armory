# Humanizer

Skill for reducing recognizable AI-writing patterns while preserving facts, voice, and intentional style.

## Use for
- AI-sounding prose cleanup
- repetitive phrasing and generic filler
- over-structured or over-polished drafts
- cadence and voice restoration

## Rules
- Never claim to determine authorship from style alone.
- Diagnose observable patterns rather than assigning an "AI score."
- Replace vague wording with the specific point, not a thesaurus synonym.
- Preserve deliberate slang, humor, fragments, and stylistic quirks when they are part of the writer's voice.
- Do not remove meaningful uncertainty or precision.
