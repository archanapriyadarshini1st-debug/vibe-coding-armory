# Proofreading

Final-pass skill for correctness, consistency, readability, and presentation quality.

## Check
- spelling and grammar
- punctuation and capitalization
- tense and agreement
- duplicated or missing words
- unclear references and awkward phrasing
- headings and formatting consistency
- numbers, dates, names, and terminology

## Rule
Do not silently change factual meaning. Flag uncertain facts instead of guessing.
