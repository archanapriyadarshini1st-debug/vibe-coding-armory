# Writing Craft

Agent skill reference for strong, human-sounding prose, editing, structure, rhythm, specificity, and anti-slop checks.

## Use for
- creative and professional drafting
- line editing and rewrites
- stronger hooks, transitions, and endings
- removing vague, inflated, repetitive, or obviously AI-shaped prose
- preserving a writer's actual voice

## Core behavior
- Prefer concrete nouns, direct verbs, and precise claims.
- Vary sentence and paragraph rhythm deliberately.
- Cut filler, needless qualifiers, throat-clearing, and generic conclusions.
- Preserve useful personality, humor, uncertainty, and domain-specific language.
- Never invent facts, sources, quotations, statistics, or opinions.
- Edit toward clarity and specificity rather than simply swapping synonyms.

## Modes
**Write:** draft from the user's purpose, audience, constraints, and desired voice.

**Edit:** identify the highest-value problems, rewrite the full passage, then briefly explain the meaningful changes.

**Audit:** identify suspicious AI-style patterns without claiming certainty about authorship.
