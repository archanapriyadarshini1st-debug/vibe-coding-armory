# General Writing

General-purpose editing skill for clear, sharp, human prose that preserves meaning and voice.

## Use for
- rewriting and polishing
- clarity, concision, grammar, and flow
- removing AI-sounding patterns
- adapting tone without flattening personality
- final house-style passes

## Rules
- Make the minimum effective edit.
- Preserve facts, intent, uncertainty, humor, and recognizable voice.
- Prefer direct verbs and concrete wording.
- Do not pad with generic introductions, summaries, or motivational filler.
- Do not invent claims or examples.
- Keep formatting purposeful.

## Workflow
1. Read the entire draft.
2. Identify audience, purpose, and desired outcome.
3. Fix meaning-breaking issues before style issues.
4. Remove repetition, vague language, filler, and mechanical phrasing.
5. Read the result for rhythm and voice.
6. Return the complete revised draft plus a short change summary when editing is requested.
