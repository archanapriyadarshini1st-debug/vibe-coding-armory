# School Answer Writing

Skill for producing school-ready answers that fit the question, marks, class level, and expected subject format.

## Use for
- textbook questions
- short/long answers
- 1/2/3/5-mark responses
- explain, compare, differentiate, give reasons, define, and describe questions

## Method
1. Identify command word and marks.
2. Extract exactly what the question asks.
3. Build the answer around the required number of ideas.
4. Match the student's grade level and textbook terminology.
5. Use headings, points, equations, examples, or diagrams only when useful/expected.
6. End without unnecessary repetition.

## Guardrails
- Do not add invented textbook facts.
- Do not turn every answer into a long essay.
- Preserve question-specific terminology.
- Optimize for correctness and marking clarity, not impressive vocabulary.
