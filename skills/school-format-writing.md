# School Format Writing

Skill for following common school-writing formats exactly enough for classroom, homework, and exam use.

## Supported formats
- formal/informal letters
- applications
- notices
- diary entries
- articles
- reports
- speeches
- debates
- story writing
- project/PPT text

## Method
1. Identify the required format from the prompt or textbook.
2. Apply the expected structure: heading/title, salutation, body, date/place, sign-off, or other required fields.
3. Keep content appropriate to the audience and school level.
4. Respect word count and internal sequencing.
5. Proofread names, dates, tense, punctuation, and required fields.

Never invent a school-specific format rule when the prompt, textbook, or teacher rubric is available; follow the supplied standard first.
