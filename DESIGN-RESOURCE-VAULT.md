## Design Standard

The final work should:

- Avoid generic AI-generated visual patterns.
- Avoid arbitrary gradients and excessive glassmorphism.
- Avoid repetitive card grids and overused bento layouts unless the content genuinely calls for them.
- Avoid excessive rounded corners and decorative effects without purpose.
- Use intentional typography, spacing, hierarchy, composition, and contrast.
- Use motion to communicate hierarchy, state, feedback, and spatial relationships.
- Prefer human-curated references when making aesthetic decisions.
- Use current official documentation before implementing third-party libraries.
- Maintain accessibility, responsiveness, performance, and production-quality code.
- Treat component libraries as sources of building blocks, not as a visual identity.
- Keep the final website visually coherent rather than looking like a showcase of unrelated libraries.
